## Triage's Stage 2 Goal

Demonstrate real adoption of Triage as a crypto-tax forensic reconciliation agent,
validate its value with qualified users, contribute measurable value to the ClawUp
ecosystem, and publish useful GEO content grounded in real implementation and user
learning.

The primary proof event is a **qualified completed reconciliation review**:

1. A target user submits a synthetic, redacted, or explicitly consented case.
2. Triage returns a cited reconstruction or workpaper.
3. The result distinguishes `RECONCILED`, `RECONCILED (assumption)`, and
   `UNRECONCILED` items.
4. The user reviews the output and records what was useful, wrong, unclear, or
   missing.
5. The review produces evidence of value, a product decision, or both.

Stage 2 is not primarily about adding more prototype features. The priority is a
usable end-to-end loop, qualified usage, structured feedback, measurable learning,
and defensible evidence.

## Product and User Context

Triage reconstructs supported wallet-by-wallet crypto cost basis across messy
multi-wallet and multi-chain histories. It connects conclusions to evidence, records
assumptions, and flags gaps instead of inventing missing basis. It produces a
reviewable workpaper; it does not file returns or replace the accountable human.

Target users:

- Primary: crypto-specialist CPA and tax firms that already perform manual cleanup.
- Secondary: DeFi-heavy individuals with multiple wallets, chains, bridges, LP
  activity, staking, old exchange records, or unresolved tax-software results.

The two segments must be analyzed separately because professionals validate buyer
workflow and review value, while individuals validate access, clarity, and direct
pain.

## Four Required Workstreams

### 1. Product Growth

Measure progress using Triage's own Stage 1 metrics. Prioritize completed tasks,
reviews, repeat intent, and verified value over impressions or general attention.

### 2. Seed-User Validation

Collect structured evidence from 10-20 seed users through problem interviews,
message-comprehension tests, product tests, surveys, or reviewed demonstrations.

Each record should capture:

- anonymized participant ID and segment;
- consent and privacy conditions;
- current workflow and last concrete problem;
- time, cost, or consequence of the problem;
- tools tried and what failed;
- task or test completed with Triage;
- what was useful, wrong, unclear, or missing;
- trust and adoption blockers;
- willingness to reuse, pilot, refer, or pay;
- the product or messaging decision changed by the evidence.

### 3. GEO Content

Publish useful content throughout Stage 2 rather than batching generic articles at
the end. Strong formats include technical READMEs, integration guides, walkthroughs,
case studies, build logs, implementation notes, and educational X threads.

Every GEO asset should:

- answer a real builder or user question;
- use clear headings and extractable structure;
- use ClawUp, Metis, GOAT, x402, and ERC-8004 terminology accurately;
- include implementation evidence, examples, screenshots, code, or measured data;
- state limitations and prerequisites;
- have a canonical URL and publication date;
- be recorded in the GEO register with outcomes where available.

### 4. ClawUp Ecosystem Growth

Use the assigned referral link in relevant ClawUp promotion and retain evidence that
it was submitted to the Growth Partner. Track attributable referrals,
subscriptions, agent starts, completed tasks, x402 activity, ERC-8004 attestations,
GOAT activity, useful community participation, and resulting conversations.

## Evidence Rules

- Do not claim adoption, accuracy, time savings, revenue, user validation, or product
  improvement without dated supporting evidence.
- Maintain one evidence-register entry for every material report claim.
- Separate planning targets, self-reported activity, verified behavior, and measured
  outcomes.
- Report impressions separately from referrals, agent runs, completions, reviews,
  transactions, and repeat intent.
- Preserve failures, corrections, limitations, and negative feedback.
- Record why a metric or target changes rather than silently rewriting it.
- Do not invent customer quotations or publish confidential case material.

## Privacy and Safety Boundaries

- Prefer synthetic cases for early tests.
- Use redacted records only with explicit consent.
- Never request seed phrases, private keys, write-capable exchange credentials,
  Social Security numbers, or unnecessary tax-return information.
- Keep private case material in ignored local folders such as `memory/jobs/`,
  `outputs/`, `reports/`, or `workpapers/`.
- Publish only aggregate, anonymized, or explicitly approved evidence.
- Payment never changes the reconciliation result.
- Triage remains read-only for client assets and does not file, sign, or provide
  certified tax advice.
